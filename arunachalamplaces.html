🛕 Arunachalam Places to See

🛕 Arunachaleswarar Temple

Famous temple dedicated to Lord Shiva (Fire element - Agni Lingam).

🚶 Girivalam Path

Sacred 14 km path around Arunachala hill for spiritual walking.

🧘 Ramana Ashram

Peaceful spiritual center associated with Sri Ramana Maharshi.

⛰ Virupaksha Cave

Ancient cave where saints meditated on Arunachala hill.

🌄 Skandashram

Beautiful hilltop ashram with scenic and peaceful surroundings.
