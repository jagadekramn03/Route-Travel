🏔 Ladakh Places to See

🌊 Pangong Lake

Famous high-altitude lake with changing blue shades.

🏜 Nubra Valley

Known for sand dunes and double-humped camels.

🏯 Leh Palace

Historic palace offering panoramic views of Leh town.

⛰ Khardung La Pass

One of the highest motorable roads in the world.

🛕 Hemis Monastery

Largest and richest monastery in Ladakh.
