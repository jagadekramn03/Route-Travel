🔥 Arunachalam

About: Arunachalam (Tiruvannamalai) is a sacred place dedicated to Lord Shiva as Arunachaleswarar, famous for Girivalam.

Best Time: November – March

Weather: Hot most of the year

Route: Reach Tiruvannamalai → Girivalam (~14 km)
