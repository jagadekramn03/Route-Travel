🏔 Spiti Valley Places to See

🏯 Key Monastery

One of the oldest and largest monasteries in Spiti Valley.

🌙 Chandratal Lake

Beautiful crescent-shaped lake known for its crystal-clear water.

🏘 Kaza

Main town of Spiti Valley and base for exploring nearby places.

📮 Hikkim Village

Home to the world’s highest post office.

🗿 Langza Village

Famous for fossils and giant Buddha statue with mountain views.
